How this quiz works

  • Seven tap-to-answer questions, one per screen. Single-choice questions move on by themselves; multi-choice ones have a Continue button.
  • The first answer picks the program: back, neck or radiating pain goes to Spine, Neck & Back Recovery; knee to Knee Restoration; shoulder to Shoulder Restoration; numbness or tingling to Neuropathy Recovery; a car accident to Personal Injury; digestion, energy or weight to Metabolic & Gut Health; anything else to a general evaluation with a link to the closest page.
  • Question 2 changes with the first answer and uses the checklists from each program page, so the result can repeat back what the visitor told us. "Something else" lists the remaining conditions the clinic treats (hip, elbow, foot and plantar fasciitis, headaches, arthritis, tendonitis, scoliosis).
  • Safety check: if a visitor ticks a warning sign (for example loss of bladder control or chest pain), the quiz shows "get checked by a doctor first" instead of booking.
  • No payment or credit questions. Payment and insurance stay on the New Patients page.
  • The booking area stands in for the clinic's scheduler (the CRM's booking tool or Calendly). The times shown are examples.
  • On the live site the answers and contact details go to the clinic's CRM, tagged with the program and how soon the visitor wants to start. Health answers next to a name and phone number are protected health information, so the CRM needs a signed HIPAA business associate agreement, and the answers must stay out of analytics and ad pixels.
  • Where it would sit: a "Take the 45-second quiz" button in the homepage hero, the "Not sure which program fits?" section on the Programs page, and this page on its own for ads.
